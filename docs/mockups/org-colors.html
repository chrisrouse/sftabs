Org Colors

A fifth tile in Settings. Everything at the real 380px tray width; swatches stand in for a native <input type="color">.

The proposal: not two modes but two layers in one sheet. Environment colors give every org a sensible color with no setup; a per-org entry overrides it. That way your three sandboxes start green together and you recolor only the ones you need to tell apart — rather than switching to "custom" and having to map every org by hand.

Two things worth knowing before we build

Only three environments are detectable. The hostname tells us production from --name.sandbox from .develop, and that is all it tells us. Full Copy, Partial Copy and Developer sandboxes are all just .sandbox. — Salesforce does not put the type in the URL. So the environment layer can honestly offer three rows, not the five that extension showed. Telling a Full Copy from a Partial Copy is exactly what the per-org layer is for.
The org key is the subdomain. extractOrgIdentifier already returns acme and acme--dev1, and it is the same key profiles match on — so "piggyback on profiles" is a join on a value we already compute, not new matching logic.
